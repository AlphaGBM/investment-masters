# Illustrative comparison: Buffett and Howard Marks

> **Fixture status:** synthetic and offline. This is not a current view from
> either investor and does not evaluate a security.

## Question

How do Buffett's value lens and Howard Marks' cycle lens differ when reviewing
a company supplied by the user?

## Methodology lens

| Lens | Primary question | Evidence to request |
|---|---|---|
| Buffett | Is the business understandable, durable and worth more than the price? | Unit economics, moat, reinvestment, balance sheet and valuation |
| Marks | Is the market pricing risk too aggressively or too cheaply? | Cycle position, expectations, downside scenarios and margin of safety |

## What this does not establish

- A methodology lens does not create a buy or sell instruction.
- A profile does not prove that a named investor currently owns the company.
- A current holding requires a dated primary filing or another named source.
