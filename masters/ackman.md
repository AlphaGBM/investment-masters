# Bill Ackman -- Pershing Square Capital Management

## One-Line Summary
Take 8-12 concentrated positions in high-quality businesses with clear catalysts, and maintain a rigorous thesis with defined exit criteria.

## Core Methodology

### Stock Selection
- **Simple, predictable businesses**: Free cash flow generative, dominant market position, pricing power
- **Catalyst-driven**: Every investment has an identifiable catalyst (management change, spin-off, regulatory shift, restructuring)
- **Thesis documentation**: Written thesis for every position with: (1) Why it's mispriced, (2) What will unlock value, (3) What would prove us wrong
- **Activist when needed**: Willing to push for change (board seats, management, strategy) to realize value

### Position Sizing
- **Ultra-concentrated**: 8-12 positions, top 3 often >50% of portfolio
- **Conviction-weighted**: Size reflects confidence in thesis and catalyst timing
- Maximum single position: historically up to 25-30% of fund

### Risk Control / Exit
- **Thesis-based exits**: Exit when (1) thesis is fully realized, (2) thesis is broken, (3) better opportunity appears
- **Hedging**: Famous 2020 COVID hedge -- $27M in CDS protection turned into $2.6B profit in 3 weeks
- **Admit mistakes publicly**: Transparent about losses (Valeant, Herbalife) and what went wrong
- **Time limit**: If catalyst doesn't materialize within expected window, reassess

## Key Framework: The Ackman Thesis Template

```markdown
## Investment Thesis: {Company}

### Why It's Mispriced
- {Market misunderstands X}
- {Consensus estimate is wrong because Y}

### Catalyst
- {What will change perception}
- {Expected timeline}

### Variant Perception
- {What we see that the market doesn't}

### Exit Criteria
- Upside target: ${X} ({Y}% upside)
- Downside limit: ${X} ({Y}% loss triggers reassessment)
- Thesis-break conditions: {list}

### Position Size
- {X}% of portfolio
- Risk budget: {X}% of NAV

### Confidence Score
- Thesis quality: {1-10}
- Catalyst probability: {1-10}
- Timing confidence: {1-10}
```

## Lessons for Your Strategy
- **Document every thesis**: Before buying, write down why, what the catalyst is, and what would prove you wrong
- **Set exit criteria upfront**: Don't wait until you're emotional to decide when to sell
- **Asymmetric hedges**: Allocate 1-2% of portfolio to tail risk protection when it's cheap
- **Confidence scoring**: Rate your conviction numerically; size accordingly
