# Duan Yongping -- H&H International Investment

## One-Line Summary
"本分" (do the right thing) plus circle of competence: buy great businesses you deeply understand, hold for the long run, and keep a "stop-doing list."

## Core Methodology

### Stock Selection
- **Business model & culture first**: Judge the durability of the business model and the integrity of corporate culture before looking at price
- **Circle of competence**: Only invest in businesses you genuinely understand; pass on everything else without regret
- **Owner mindset**: Buy a stock as if buying the whole company -- think in decades, not quarters
- **本分 (honesty/integrity)**: Invest only in companies whose culture and management he respects
- Famously holds a very large Apple position; also associated with Moutai, NetEase, Berkshire, and Google

### Position Sizing
- **Highly concentrated**: Willing to put a very large share of capital into the highest-conviction idea
- Size follows depth of understanding, not diversification targets

### Risk Control / Exit
- **"Stop-doing list" (不为清单)**: No leverage, no shorting, no acting outside the circle of competence, nothing against 本分
- Defines risk as doing things you don't understand -- not short-term volatility
- Rarely sells a great business; holds through drawdowns as long as the business is intact

## Lessons for Your Strategy
- **Understand before you size**: Conviction must come from understanding the business, not from a chart
- **A "stop-doing list" beats a to-do list**: Most damage comes from doing the wrong things, not from missing the right ones
- **Culture is a moat**: Management integrity and corporate culture compound over decades

## AlphaGBM Systematic Equivalent
- Business-quality score: ROE, margin stability, cash-flow durability, governance flags
- "Circle of competence" filter: restrict the universe to understandable sectors/business models
- Long-horizon hold logic: ignore volatility, exit only on a thesis or quality break
