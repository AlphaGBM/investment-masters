# Zhang Lei -- Hillhouse Capital Management

## One-Line Summary
Long-term value creation through deep research, concentrated bets on structural winners, and patient capital in China and global markets.

## Core Methodology

### Investment Philosophy
- **Long-termism**: "The best arbitrage is time arbitrage" -- most investors are too short-term, creating opportunities for patient capital
- **Research depth**: Hillhouse's due diligence is legendary -- hundreds of hours of primary research per investment
- **Structural winners**: Invest in companies that benefit from irreversible structural trends (digitalization, consumption upgrade, healthcare reform in China)
- **Value creation, not extraction**: Partner with management to build long-term value, not strip assets

### Stock Selection
- **Industry structure**: Invest in industries with favorable competitive dynamics (oligopoly, network effects, regulatory moats)
- **Management quality**: Founder-led companies with missionary (not mercenary) leadership
- **China + Global**: Deep expertise in Chinese market dynamics + global perspective for comparison
- **Full lifecycle**: Invest from early stage (venture) through growth to public markets -- unique multi-stage view

### Position Sizing
- **Concentrated**: Top 10-15 positions represent majority of portfolio
- **High conviction, long duration**: Average holding period 3-5+ years
- **Cross-stage**: Same company from Series B through IPO and beyond

### Risk Control / Exit
- **Exit when structural thesis breaks**: Not when price drops -- when the competitive landscape changes
- **Patience through volatility**: Willing to hold through 50%+ drawdowns if thesis intact
- **Team-based decision making**: Investment committee with diverse backgrounds reduces individual bias

## Lessons for Your Strategy
- **Time arbitrage**: If everyone is focused on next quarter, focus on next 3 years
- **Research depth over breadth**: Knowing 10 companies deeply beats knowing 100 superficially
- **China is not optional**: For a globally diversified portfolio, China exposure is structural -- not a trade
- **Founders matter**: Invest behind missionary founders, not hired-gun CEOs
