# Linda Raschke -- LBRGroup

## One-Line Summary
Pick quantifiable short-term patterns, define risk before size, cut instantly when wrong -- a 40-year career built on mechanical execution.

## Core Methodology

### Pattern Selection
- **Markets are range-bound 80% of the time**: Short-term mean-reversion is the base rate; trending is the exception
- **Three canonical patterns** (from *Street Smarts*, 1996):
  - **Opening Range Breakout (ORB)**: Enter when price breaks the first 15-30 min range, stop inside the range
  - **Turtle Soup**: Fade false breakouts of 20-day highs/lows -- most breakouts fail
  - **80-20 pattern**: Reversal setup when a bar opens in top/bottom 20% and closes in opposite 20%
- **Repeatable + measurable** -- no "gut feel" patterns allowed in the toolkit

### Position Sizing
- **Risk first, size second**: Decide max $ loss per trade, then back into position size
- Typical per-trade risk: 0.5% to 2% of account depending on pattern win rate
- Multiple small pattern trades rather than concentrated bets

### Risk Control / Exit
- **Stops are non-negotiable**: Mechanical execution, no "I think it'll bounce"
- **Quick cuts**: Short-term patterns don't allow the forgiveness long-term positions do
- **Scale out winners in tranches**: Book partial profits at defined levels
- **Daily risk cap**: Stop trading for the day after a fixed $ loss -- prevents tilt compounding

## Lessons for Your Strategy
- **Mechanical beats discretionary** for short-term: define 3-5 patterns with exact entry/stop/target, never deviate
- **Survive first, earn second**: Most blow-ups happen in the first 3 years -- if you make it to year 4 with your account intact, you've passed the hardest filter
- **Daily routine > big wins**: Process focus > outcome focus; journal every trade against its pattern rules
- **Patience to sit out**: Not every day has a setup -- forcing trades is the #1 career killer

## AlphaGBM Systematic Equivalent
- **Pre-defined pattern library**: Instead of "I'll decide when I see the chart", codify specific rule-based entries
- **Per-trade risk budget**: 1% fixed $ loss cap per trade; position size derived from stop distance
- **Session rules**: Max 3 trades / day, stop after 2 losers -- encoded as hard limits, not suggestions
