# Linda Raschke -- LBRGroup

## One-Line Summary
Short-term technical trading with strict risk control: read market structure and momentum, take high-probability setups, and protect capital relentlessly.

## Core Methodology

### Selection / Timing
- **Technical, market-structure based**: Trade price action, momentum, and volatility patterns -- not fundamentals
- **Defined setups**: A repeatable library of patterns (momentum pullbacks, the "Holy Grail," 80-20 bars, range breakouts)
- **Short holding periods**: Day-to-swing trades, typically hours to a few days
- **Edge from probability + execution**: No single trade matters; the edge is positive expectancy applied consistently
- **Liquidity focus**: Trades liquid futures and equities where execution is clean

### Position Sizing
- **Small, consistent risk per trade**: Fixed-fractional risk -- never let one trade threaten the account
- Scale into confirmed moves; avoid oversizing any single idea

### Risk Control / Exit
- **Hard stops, always**: "The whole secret to winning is in the management of losses" -- the exit is defined before the entry
- **Cut losers fast, let winners reach targets**: discipline over prediction
- **Process over outcome**: track expectancy and consistency, not individual trade P&L

## Why 13F Doesn't Help
Raschke is a short-term technical trader (LBRGroup); positions turn over in hours to days and are not disclosed via 13F. Her methodology lives in her books, interviews, and decades of trading.

## Recent Views
- Emphasizes that routine, consistency, and risk discipline -- not prediction -- separate professionals from amateurs
- Profiled in *The New Market Wizards* as one of the few women featured

## Lessons for Your Strategy
- **Define the exit before the entry**: Risk is decided up front, not in the heat of the moment
- **Expectancy beats accuracy**: A modest win rate with good risk/reward compounds; chasing high accuracy does not
- **Trade a few repeatable setups**, not everything that moves

## AlphaGBM Systematic Equivalent
- Technical signal library: momentum / pullback / breakout patterns with backtested expectancy
- Fixed-fractional position sizing with mandatory stop placement
- Trade-journal and expectancy tracking instead of outcome-chasing
