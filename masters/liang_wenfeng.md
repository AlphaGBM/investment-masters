# Liang Wenfeng -- High-Flyer (幻方量化) + DeepSeek

## One-Line Summary
Reinvest every profit into R&D, refuse external capital, and let independence compound over 5-10 year horizons.

## Core Methodology

### Capital Allocation
- **R&D-first**: Reinvest the majority of quant profits into research infrastructure instead of distributing dividends
- **No external fundraising**: High-Flyer and DeepSeek both rejected outside capital to avoid short-term performance pressure
- **No IPO**: Staying private preserves the tempo required for genuinely long-term projects
- **Talent over structure**: Hire PhDs (especially from Tsinghua, Zhejiang University, and top physics programs) over MBAs -- bet on technical depth

### Opportunity Recognition
- **Bet before consensus forms**: In 2021, bought 10,000+ NVIDIA A100 GPUs when AI hype was modest -- by the time export controls + AI boom arrived, the stockpile was a moat
- **Constraint-driven innovation**: When US export controls limited GPU access, DeepSeek forced algorithmic efficiency -- shipped V3 / R1 at a fraction of OpenAI's cost with comparable quality
- **Quant profits as venture capital**: Treat the hedge fund as a self-funding engine for ambitious R&D projects

### Operational Principles
- Quant strategy and AI research share the same team philosophy: fundamental research + infrastructure discipline
- Ship papers publicly alongside products (DeepSeek's open weights + published training recipes)
- Career incentives are internal: career growth comes from research contribution, not client AUM chasing

## Lessons for Your Strategy
- **Reinvestment ratio**: Commit a fixed fraction of gains to R&D -- skills, tools, infrastructure -- not just profits to withdraw
- **Independence premium**: Don't borrow capital that forces you to adopt someone else's time horizon
- **Early-stage pattern match**: "It feels too early" is often when the asymmetry is biggest
- **Constraints breed creativity**: Small capital isn't a disadvantage -- it forces you to actually understand what you're doing

## AlphaGBM Systematic Equivalent
- **Long-horizon portfolio design**: Set a 3-5 year rolling target, ignore quarterly drawdowns
- **Reinvestment budget**: Treat some % of gains as "forced R&D spend" -- education, tools, new strategy research
- **Independence check**: Refuse leverage, refuse trade ideas from sources that would pressure your exit timing
