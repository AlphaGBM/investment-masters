# Liang Wenfeng -- High-Flyer (幻方量化) / DeepSeek

## One-Line Summary
Engineer-driven quantitative investing: use AI, large datasets, and full automation to extract signal -- then reinvest the edge into fundamental AI research.

## Core Methodology

### Selection / Timing
- **Pure quant**: Machine-learning models over price, volume, and alternative data -- no discretionary stock-picking or macro calls
- **Full automation**: Strategies execute systematically across thousands of A-share names with minimal human intervention
- **AI-first infrastructure**: Built large GPU clusters for research; that same deep-learning capability later spun into DeepSeek (open-source LLMs)
- **Engineering culture**: Hires researchers and engineers rather than traditional traders; treats investing as a research problem

### Position Sizing
- **Model-driven**: Position sizes set by signal strength and a risk budget, not by conviction or narrative
- **Massive diversification**: Many small, decorrelated bets rather than a few large ones

### Risk Control / Exit
- **Embedded in the models**: Signals are dropped automatically as they decay; risk is monitored continuously
- **Capacity awareness**: Quant edge erodes with size, so capital and strategy capacity are actively managed
- Operates within China A-share regulatory constraints on quant/high-frequency activity

## Why 13F Doesn't Help
High-Flyer (幻方) is a China-based quant manager trading primarily A-shares; it does not file a US SEC 13F, and positions turn over far too quickly for quarterly snapshots to be useful. Liang is now best known as the founder of DeepSeek.

## Recent Views
- Argues China should move from "application" to fundamental innovation in AI; DeepSeek open-sourced competitive LLMs at a fraction of typical training cost
- Frames quant investing and AGI research as the same pursuit: turning data and compute into a durable edge

## Lessons for Your Strategy
- **If you can't backtest it, don't trade it**: Reproducibility and discipline over intuition
- **Many small decorrelated bets** beat a few large ones for risk-adjusted return
- **Reinvest the edge into capability**: Compounding infrastructure -- data, compute, talent -- is a durable advantage

## AlphaGBM Systematic Equivalent
- Multi-signal quant scoring with automatic decay and regime detection
- Risk-budgeted sizing across a broad, decorrelated universe
- Backtest-gated deployment: nothing trades that hasn't been validated first
