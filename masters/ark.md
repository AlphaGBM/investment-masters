# Cathie Wood -- ARK Invest

## One-Line Summary
Bet big on disruptive innovation platforms -- AI, robotics, genomics, blockchain, energy storage -- with extreme conviction and radical transparency.

## Core Methodology

### Investment Philosophy
- **Disruptive innovation**: Invest in technologies at the inflection point of S-curve adoption
- **5 innovation platforms**: AI, robotics, energy storage, blockchain, multi-omic sequencing
- **Wright's Law over Moore's Law**: Cost declines follow cumulative production (every doubling of units = X% cost decline)
- **Radical transparency**: Publishes daily trades, open-source models, annual Big Ideas report
- **Long-term conviction**: Willing to hold through 70%+ drawdowns if innovation thesis intact

### Stock Selection
- **TAM obsession**: Total addressable market must be enormous ($1T+)
- **Innovation convergence**: Companies benefiting from multiple platforms simultaneously score highest
- **Revenue inflection**: Look for companies approaching hockey-stick adoption curves
- **Management vision**: Founders and CEOs who think in decades, not quarters

### Position Sizing
- **Concentrated**: ARK flagship ARKK holds 30-50 positions, top 10 often >50% of fund
- **Conviction-weighted**: Size proportional to innovation score and TAM
- **Active management**: Frequent rebalancing, adding on dips, trimming on rips
- **Public daily trades**: Every trade published daily -- unprecedented transparency

### Risk Control / Exit
- **No stop-losses**: ARK famously adds to losers if the innovation thesis is intact
- **Sell when adoption peaks**: Exit when technology moves from exponential growth to linear growth
- **Sell on thesis break**: If competitive dynamics change fundamentally
- **Volatility is a feature, not a bug**: "Innovation is inherently volatile" -- accepts 50%+ drawdowns

## Lessons for Your Strategy (Positive AND Negative)

### What to Learn
- **Think in S-curves**: Understand technology adoption curves for timing entry
- **Radical transparency builds trust**: Publishing trades/models builds community and AUM
- **Conviction matters**: If thesis is right, drawdowns are buying opportunities

### What to Avoid
- **No risk management is itself a risk**: Adding to -70% losers works until it doesn't
- **Narrative over numbers**: "Big TAM" without near-term cash flow is speculation, not investment
- **Single-factor exposure**: ARKK was effectively a leveraged bet on low interest rates
- **Survivorship bias**: The winning bets (TSLA in 2020) overshadow the many losers
