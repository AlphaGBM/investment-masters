# Stanley Druckenmiller -- Duquesne (Family Office)

## One-Line Summary
Top-down macro plus bottom-up timing: make big, concentrated, asymmetric bets when conviction is high -- and preserve capital fiercely the rest of the time.

## Core Methodology

### Macro Bets / Selection
- **Liquidity-driven**: Central-bank policy and liquidity, not current earnings, move markets -- "earnings don't move the overall market; the Fed moves the market"
- **Asymmetry**: Seek setups where downside is contained and upside is large
- **Top-down + bottom-up**: Form a macro thesis, then express it through the best instrument (currency, bond, equity, or commodity)
- **Forward-looking**: Position for where fundamentals will be in 6-18 months, not where they are now
- Partnered with Soros to "break the Bank of England" (1992)

### Position Sizing
- **Highly concentrated, dynamically scaled**: Few large positions; sizes up aggressively only on high conviction
- "The way to build long-term returns is through preservation of capital and home runs"
- Uses leverage and derivatives to express macro views efficiently

### Risk Control / Exit
- **Capital preservation first**: roughly three decades at Duquesne Capital with no losing year
- **Cut fast when wrong**: reverse a position entirely when the macro picture changes -- never marry a trade
- Bet size is the lever: be right or wrong small, but bet big only when the edge is clear

## Lessons for Your Strategy
- **Bet size is the edge**: How much you make when right and how little you lose when wrong matters more than hit rate
- **Follow liquidity**: Central-bank policy often dominates fundamentals over 6-18 months
- **Defense enables offense**: No down years come from relentless capital preservation between the home runs
