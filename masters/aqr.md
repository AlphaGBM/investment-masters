# Cliff Asness -- AQR Capital Management

## One-Line Summary
Markets are mostly efficient but systematically mispriced along known risk factors -- harvest these premia through disciplined, diversified, rules-based investing.

## Core Methodology

### Investment Philosophy
- **Factor investing**: Returns can be decomposed into exposure to systematic risk factors (value, momentum, carry, defensive/quality)
- **Academic rigor**: Every strategy must have (1) economic rationale, (2) statistical evidence across markets and time periods, (3) implementation feasibility
- **Diversification is the only free lunch**: Diversify across factors, asset classes, and geographies
- **Behavioral finance**: Many factor premia exist because of human cognitive biases (overreaction, anchoring, herding)

### Key Factors

| Factor | Definition | Why It Works |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **Value** | Buy cheap, sell expensive (P/B, P/E, EV/EBITDA) | Mean reversion + behavioral overreaction to bad news |
| **Momentum** | Buy recent winners, sell recent losers (12-1 month returns) | Underreaction to information + herding |
| **Carry** | Buy high-yield assets, sell low-yield (dividend yield, bond yield) | Compensation for risk |
| **Defensive/Quality** | Buy low-beta, high-quality; sell high-beta, low-quality | Leverage aversion + lottery preference |

### Position Sizing
- **Risk-weighted**: Equal risk contribution across factors, not equal dollar
- **Volatility targeting**: Scale positions to target a specific portfolio volatility
- **Long-short**: Most factor strategies are long-short to isolate the factor from market beta
- **Rebalance systematically**: Monthly or quarterly, rules-based, no discretionary overrides

### Risk Control / Exit
- **No single-factor concentration**: Value can underperform for years (2018-2020 value drawdown was brutal for AQR)
- **Blended factor portfolio**: Combining value + momentum + quality + carry smooths returns
- **Maximum position limits**: No single position > X% of portfolio
- **Drawdown monitoring**: Reduce leverage if portfolio drawdown exceeds threshold

## Performance
- AQR manages ~$100B across institutional strategies
- 2018-2020 was painful: value factor had historic drawdown
- Long-term (20+ years): factor premia have delivered, but with significant variation in timing

## Why 13F Doesn't Fully Help
AQR's 13F shows long equity positions from their registered funds, but misses:
- Short positions (critical for long-short factor strategies)
- Futures and derivatives exposure
- Non-equity factor positions (bonds, currencies, commodities)

## Lessons for Your Strategy
- **Factor diversification**: Don't bet on one factor alone; combine value + momentum + quality
- **Stick with it**: Factor premia are real but lumpy. The biggest risk is abandoning the strategy during drawdowns
- **Rules over discretion**: Human overrides destroy systematic edge
- **Rebalance matters**: Systematic rebalancing harvests mean reversion
