# Warren Buffett -- Berkshire Hathaway

## One-Line Summary
Buy wonderful companies at fair prices, hold forever, and let compounding do the work.

## Core Methodology

### Stock Selection
- **Moat**: Only invest in businesses with durable competitive advantages (brand, network effects, switching costs, cost advantages, regulatory barriers)
- **Understandable**: "Never invest in a business you cannot understand" -- stays within circle of competence
- **Quality management**: Honest, capable management with skin in the game
- **Strong financials**: High ROE (>15%), consistent earnings growth, low debt, strong free cash flow
- **Margin of safety**: Buy below intrinsic value -- the gap is your safety net

### Position Sizing
- **Concentrated**: Top 5 positions often represent 60-70% of portfolio
- **"Put all your eggs in one basket, and watch that basket"**
- Size proportional to conviction -- highest-conviction ideas get largest allocation
- Berkshire's cash pile is itself a position: optionality for extreme opportunities

### Risk Control / Exit
- **Rarely sells**: "Our favorite holding period is forever"
- Sells when: (1) thesis breaks, (2) better opportunity with much higher expected return, (3) management quality deteriorates
- Risk is **permanent capital loss**, not volatility
- Avoids leverage on equity positions
- Uses insurance float as "free" leverage instead

## Lessons for Your Strategy
- **Quantify the moat**: ROE > 15%, consistent 5yr earnings growth > 10%, debt-to-equity < 0.5
- **Concentration works if you do the work**: Don't over-diversify; 8-15 positions is plenty
- **Time horizon matters**: Hold through drawdowns if thesis is intact -- selling on volatility destroys compounding
